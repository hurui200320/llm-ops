# 20261105 GLM-5.3-Flash IQ2_S on maya: speed tuning (pp/tg)

Model/lane: `glm-5.3-flash-iq2s-derisked` (Maya-M-Derisked IQ2_S, 116 GB, 3 shards) on the Maya GLM engine (`maya`
1.1.53, HIP/gfx1100), 2x RX 7900 XTX (bring-up: [notes/maya.md](../maya.md) 20261105). Target: raise pp/tg while keeping
**fp16 KV, 512k context (`524288`)**, and runtime VRAM headroom **>=500 MiB on the monitor GPU (HIP `CUDA0` / sysfs
`card1`) and >=300 MiB on the other GPU (`CUDA1` / sysfs `card0`) during inference**.

## Method and A0 baseline mechanics

- **Method**: One-shot arm containers (`127.0.0.1:10110`, llama-swap stopped) on an isolated copy of the lane tree
  (`/mnt/unenc-xfs/maya/glm53f-iq2s-derisked-tune`, read-only per-file shard binds, slots wiped) with per-arm configs in
  `/mnt/unenc-xfs/maya/tune/glm-arm-configs/<arm>/`. Measured with `bench/speed/run_longctx.py --model
  glm-5.3-flash-iq2s-derisked --ctx 524288 --in-pcts 40,85 --out-pct 1` (`cache_n 0` on every rep; `/tokenize` avoids
  calibration prefills) plus a 5 s sampler on `mem_info_vram_used`, `MemAvailable`, and `SwapFree`.
- **A0 baseline (`MAYA_GLM_RESERVE_MB=3072 MAYA_GLM_RAM_GB=36 MAYA_GLM_RAM_HEADROOM_GB=16 MAYA_GLM_PREFILL_SUB=1024
  MAYA_GLM_PREFILL_MB=4096 MAYA_GLM_SLOTS=4 MAYA_GLM_SLOT_GB=16 MAYA_GLM_SLOT_KEEP=1`, `--expert-cache auto --prefill
  auto --kv-streaming on`)**:
  - **Placement**: Auto split `CUDA0 [0,24) / CUDA1 [24,45)+head+MTP` (predicted 60.4 ms/tok: CUDA0 57.9, CUDA1 60.4).
    Expert pools `16.40 + 16.50 GB` = **4,034 VRAM experts** (`2,029 + 2,005`, 59% routed); RAM tier `17.57 + 18.41 GB`
    (`2,178 + 2,245` slots = 4,423 slots, 8,308 warm in ~13 s); CPU lane 8 threads/card (x4 card PCIe expert 1.230 ms vs
    0.175 ms CPU, taking up to 7/8 RAM-tier experts on CPU). Chunk **8192** (borrows 2,659 MB: activations 2,444,
    scratch 96, staging 103). KV streaming keeps 32,768 of 524,288 positions across 6 DSA layers in VRAM (0.20 GB) +
    3.22 GB pinned/card; dense weights 3.53/3.32 GB; state/KV 0.69/0.68 GB; NextN MTP (`blk.45`) 173 MB dense on CUDA1
    (drafts over 98,741 of 154,880 tokens).
  - **Decode is fetch-bound, prefill is split-bound**: `glm stat: decode 59-66 ms/tok | vram hit 66-68 % | ram fetches
    14-16/tok | disk 16-18 reads/tok in 11-13 waits/tok, 33-37 ms/tok | promotions 25-29/tok | cpu lane 91-96
    experts/tok in 33-34 ms/tok`. Prefill at 40% (147,456 tok, 18 chunks): CUDA0 127.8 s vs CUDA1 168.7 s; at 85%
    (393,216 tok, 48 chunks): CUDA0 393.9 s vs CUDA1 484.3 s (the x4 card is 23–32% slower per stage, streaming chunk
    experts at 6.3 GB/s vs CUDA0's 16.3 GB/s). Because A0 left **2,244 / 2,716 MiB** free (`MemAvailable` 11.7 GiB, swap
    flat), the pass was reordered to claw back VRAM reserves first before testing compute/fetch knobs.

## Context cap (`384k`), RAM tier relief (`32`/`30 GB`), and `--kv int8` (deployed)

- **Context cap `512k` → `384k` (`--max-context 393216`, rejected)**: Shrinks VRAM state/KV (`0.69/0.68` → `0.59/0.58 GB`)
  and pinned latent cache (`3.22` → `2.42 GB/card`), adding only **+10 VRAM experts** (`4,517` → `4,527`, +0.2%) with
  unchanged pp/tg at equal token length. Not worth losing 128k usable context.
- **RAM tier relief (`MAYA_GLM_RAM_GB=36` vs `32` vs `30`, 1 rep, fp16 KV)**:

  | **30** | 901.0 / 932.0 | 17.4 / 19.3 (−3.5%) | 15.0/tok / 30.6 ms | **16.2 GiB** | flat |

- **`--kv int8` (deployed 2026-11-06; default in Maya 1.1.55+)**: Halves the pinned latent cache (`3.22` → **1.71
  GB/card**, 544 B/pos/layer) and VRAM KV window (`0.20` → **0.11 GB/card**, state/KV `0.60/0.59 GB`, `4,527` VRAM

  because slot identity does not record KV dtype.

# 20261109 MAYA_GLM_PCIE_SHARE + MAYA_GLM_READ_CHUNKS (C-arms): short-decode wins, both reverted

  `MAYA_GLM_PCIE_SHARE` pins a **global** share on both cards and **disables `CPU_RETIME`** (`src/core/glm_fast_path.cu`).
